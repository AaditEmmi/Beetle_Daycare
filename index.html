Letter to Parents

Simple guidance for a comfortable day

Diapers

Most parents bring one large package a time. We will store them and let you know when they need more. Your baby will be changed every two hours or as needed.

Wipes

A package of wipes will last your baby about two weeks. We can store these as well if you purchase a large package. We will also let you know when your baby will need more.

Bottles

You will need to supply pre-made bottle each day your baby is with us. Four or five bottles (depending on your baby’s appetite and length of stay each day) should be adequate. All used and unused bottles will be sent home each day to be washed/sanitized.

Formula/Food

We must have extra formula or breast milk available to us for emergencies. All food formulas should be prepared by parents. Lunch is also prepared by parents if the your child is 1 year old or more, or is already eating all table foods for lunch.

Cups

Once your baby has started using a “sippy” cup, please send three each day that your baby is with us. These will also be sent home each day to be washed/sanitized.

Shoes

Please bring indoor shoes and socks for your baby every day they come to day care.

Clothes/Bibs

Two extra outfits should be sufficient for any spit-ups or diaper leaks or any other unforeseen messiness. Bibs are great for the teething or drooling phase.

Blanket

You should provide sheets for your baby's crib. You have to launder these items at the end of each week.

Nuk or Pacifier

One nuk is fine but two are better if your baby uses one regularly. We will keep at least one labeled nuk in our class room.

Diaper Cream

OTC diaper rash ointment or a prescription cream (with a medication form) can be used.

Baby Photo

Please bring one baby or family photo for us to display in our class room.
