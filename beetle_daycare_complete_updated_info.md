# Overview

Beetle Day Care is a licensed daycare center for infants and toddlers located in Minneapolis, Minnesota. The daycare focuses on providing a “home away from home” environment for children.

Main philosophy statement from the website:

> “Every child deserves to be at a happy and healthy home.”

The daycare emphasizes:
- Safe childcare
- Healthy environment
- Learning and play
- Parent collaboration
- Comfort and hygiene for children
